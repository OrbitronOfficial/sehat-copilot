How it works

A report arrives. Here is what happens.

  1. 9:02

    You photograph the lab report.

    Take a photo or add a PDF. Printed reports read best.

  2. 9:03

    Every value is read, with the line it came from.

    Medicines, doses, test values and dates are pulled out, each linked to its line in your document.

  3. 9:03

    Software checks each value against the range printed on the report.

    Plain code, not the AI, decides what is high or low. Lines it cannot read are listed, not guessed.

  4. 9:04

    You read it in plain words, and it joins your timeline.

    Each value outside its range gets a short explanation, and your trends update on their own.

Careful by design.

A health tool should be honest about what it does not know.

Code decides what is high or low

Ranges come from your report and fixed rules. The AI explains values. It never decides which ones are abnormal.

Nothing is guessed

Lines the reader cannot make out are listed for you to check. They are never filled in.

Handwriting is always marked for checking

Handwritten documents are read too, but every value is marked so you can compare it with the original.

Emergency and medicine-change questions get a fixed, safe reply

The same careful message every time, written by people. It is never improvised by AI.

Questions people ask.

Is my data uploaded?

Documents are sent to third-party AI services only so they can be read. Our server stores nothing. Your records stay on your device.

Is this medical advice?

No. Sehat Copilot explains what is written in your records. It is not a diagnosis, and it does not replace your doctor.

Which documents work?

Photos, PDFs and text. Printed documents read best. Handwritten ones are read too, but every value is marked for you to check.

Do I need an account?

No. The demo and guest mode work without one. An account is optional, and it is kept in your browser on this device.

Why is it free?

It is a student prototype, so it is free to use for now. Paid plans are on the roadmap, and nothing is charged today.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. From the app you can delete any single record, or everything at once.
