What it does

From a crumpled page to something you can follow.

Medical documents are written for other doctors. Sehat Copilot rewrites them for you, and shows its work.

Reads the document

Pulls out medicines, doses, test values and dates from photos, PDFs and text. Each item comes with a confidence score.

Explains in plain language

For every value outside its range, a short note on what it measures and what being high or low can mean.

One timeline, one profile

Every report you add lands on a single timeline, with trends for the values that matter to you.

Traceable to the source

Every value links back to the exact line it was read from, so you can check it against your original.

Safety first

Plain code, not the AI, decides what counts as high or low. Emergency and medicine-change questions get fixed, safe replies.

ABDM-ready

Export your records as a FHIR R4 bundle. The ABHA link is a mock for now, to show how it would connect.

How it works

Three steps, and you stay in charge.

Upload

Add a photo, PDF or text of a prescription, lab report or discharge summary.

Check against the original

See what was read next to your document. Fix anything that looks wrong before it is saved.

Understand

Get plain-language explanations, a timeline and trends, in the language you choose.

Saathi, your guide

A helper that stays with you on every screen.

Saathi answers questions about the record you are looking at and points you to where a value came from. It will not guess at a diagnosis.

A guide to your records. Not a doctor.
