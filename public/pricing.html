Questions

Before you upload anything.

Is my data uploaded?

Documents are sent to third-party AI services only so they can be read. Our server stores nothing. Your records stay on your device.

Is this medical advice?

No. Sehat Copilot explains what is written in your records. It is not a diagnosis, and it is not a substitute for your doctor.

Which documents work?

Photos, PDFs and text. Printed documents work best. Handwritten ones are read too, but are marked for you to check.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. From the app you can delete any single record, or everything at once.

Sehat Copilot explains your records. It is not a diagnosis. Always confirm with your doctor.
